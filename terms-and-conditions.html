2. Nature of Artisanal Handcraft, Natural Hides, and Patina Evolution

Every purse created at ExoticPurseAsh is assembled by hand from authentic full-grain vegetable-tanned hides and sand-cast brass hardware that naturally reflect the biological individuality of living materials. Patrons acknowledge that full-grain hides inevitably display natural neck wrinkles, healed range markings, subtle fat ripples, and grain variations that are not defects, but the hallmark proof of un-sanded authenticity. Furthermore, because our vegetable-tanned ash leather is colored with organic tannins, it will naturally evolve over time, developing a deeper, lustrous amber-tinged patina with exposure to sunlight, hand contact, and environmental air, embodying the authentic soul of genuine luxury leathercraft.

3. Bespoke Commission Schedules, Deposits, and Cancellation Protocols

To ensure flawless material allocation and preserve the concentrated bench hours required for hand saddle-stitching, bespoke commissions require a non-refundable fifty percent deposit at the moment of commission confirmation. Because our craftsmen immediately cut into individual hides and cast dedicated brass hardware tailored to your specific ergonomic requests, cancellations must be formally submitted within seventy-two hours of deposit confirmation to receive a partial refund minus administrative patterning fees. Cancellations requested after seventy-two hours, or once leather cutting has commenced on the workbench, will result in complete forfeiture of the deposit to cover expended artisan labor and dedicated raw materials.

4. Lifetime Structural Craftsmanship Warranty and Repair Service

ExoticPurseAsh proudly guarantees every hand saddle-stitched seam and solid sand-cast brass fitting against structural failure for the natural lifetime of the original registered collector. In the rare event that a hand-stitched linen thread breaks under normal recreational use or a brass rivet loosens, our atelier will repair or re-stitch the affected area free of charge upon inspection at our New York workshop. This warranty does not cover damages resulting from catastrophic accidents, unauthorized chemical cleanings, pet chewing, liquid submergence, or normal surface scuffing that naturally accumulates upon full-grain leather surfaces during regular daily carry.
